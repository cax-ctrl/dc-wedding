Privacy Policy

Your contact details are for managing this celebration. A request to attend is not consent to receive business promotions.

Updated 22 November 2026. The RSVP collection service is not yet enabled; the organiser's legal contact and retention schedule will be confirmed before it opens.

Who is responsible?

Deborah and Caleb's wedding organisers manage the guest list and decide who receives a confirmed invitation. The identity and contact details of the person or organisation legally responsible for RSVP data will be published before online RSVP collection begins. HubX Limited may support administration or technology if separately appointed; its exact role has not yet been confirmed.

Information collected

If the RSVP service is activated and you submit a request, it asks for your name, country, active mobile number, preferred confirmation method, and WeChat ID if you choose WeChat. The private organiser register may also record when you submitted, review and confirmation status, a personal invitation code, message delivery records, and venue check-in status. If gift reporting or sponsorship intake is enabled, we may also collect a guest's name, contact email, gift title, declared amount, currency and optional transfer reference, or a sponsor's name, email, preferred payment details and optional logo. Uploaded sponsor logos are kept private pending approval. Hosting and security services may process basic connection data, including IP addresses and request details.

Why it is used

Wedding details are used to review requests, manage the limited guest capacity, contact you about attendance by your chosen method, issue codes to approved guests, help manage admission and respond to enquiries. Submitting your details does not guarantee a place. Gift notices are used to match reported transfers to receipts; a notice is not evidence that funds arrived. Sponsorship requests are used to review proposed financial support, contact sponsors and agree any approved logo placement.

We do not use the RSVP form to automatically add you to a HubX Limited contact or marketing list. Any later proposal to use your number for HubX updates or services will require a separately explained, optional choice where applicable. You may decline that choice and still request a wedding invitation.

Who may see the information?

Authorised wedding organisers may view RSVP details in a restricted Google Sheet or equivalent private register. Approved entrance personnel should see only the details needed to verify invitations and check in guests. The hosting provider, secure RSVP gateway, Google services and your selected messaging platform may process limited information to provide those services. Guest phone numbers and invitation codes are not published on this website.

International processing

This invitation is intended for guests in Nigeria, the United Kingdom, the United States and China. The final hosting and message-service arrangements may involve processing outside the country where a guest lives. Before RSVP collection opens, the organisers will confirm the relevant service providers, processing locations and any information or safeguards required for international transfers.

Keeping and protecting information

Guest details should be accessible only to authorised organisers and retained only as long as necessary for wedding administration, appropriate follow-up and applicable legal requirements. A specific deletion timetable will be set before collection begins. Reasonable access restrictions and security measures should be used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to correct an inaccurate number, change your preferred confirmation channel or withdraw a request. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to use of your personal information, withdraw consent, and raise a complaint with a data protection authority. For now, contact the wedding organiser through the person who shared this invitation. A direct privacy contact will be added before RSVPs open.
